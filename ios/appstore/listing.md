## Description
Zyvor Note is a calm, fast notes app for people who want to own their words.

Every note is a plain Markdown file in a folder you choose: the app's own folder, or iCloud Drive so your notes follow you to your Mac. No account, no sign-in, no tracking, no ads. Close the app any time and your notes are still just files.

WRITE
• Open a note in a clean reading view; tap to edit
• Formatting bar above the keyboard: lists, tasks, headings, links, bold, italic, code
• Auto-pairing brackets and quotes; paste a link over selected text
• Live Preview (beta): see your Markdown rendered as you type

CONNECT
• Link notes with [[wikilinks]], jump to headings and blocks
• Backlinks, outgoing links and a live graph of how your notes connect
• Tags, properties and folders; drag notes between folders

FIND
• Instant search with phrases, tag:, path: and exclusions; save your searches
• Quick switcher, daily notes and templates

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
• Bottom tab bar, bottom sheets, edge swipe for the sidebar, long-press for actions
• Light and dark mode, haptics, and a layout that fits around the keyboard

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works fully offline; nothing leaves your device unless you pick a cloud folder
• Open source, with desktop apps for Mac, Windows and Linux
