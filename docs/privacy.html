Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Plainnote (published on the App Store as Zyvor Note) is a notes app. Your notes are plain Markdown files that stay on your devices, in a folder you choose. The short version: Plainnote does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. Plainnote has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party software development kits. The developer receives no information about you or your notes.

Where your notes are stored

Notes are files in a folder on your device: the app's own folder (visible in the Files app) or any folder you pick, such as iCloud Drive. If you choose a folder in iCloud Drive or another cloud service, that service syncs your files under its own terms and privacy policy. Plainnote itself never uploads anything.

Network use

The iPhone and iPad app works without any network connection and makes no network requests. The desktop and web versions are open-source programs you run yourself; they listen only on your own computer unless you deliberately turn on remote mode, in which case access is protected by a secret token that you control.

Permissions

Plainnote asks for access only to the folder you select. It does not use your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or health data.

Children

Because Plainnote collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes it will be updated on this page, with a new date above.
